Resolution Protocol

How a Request is Handled

01
Submit
Fill out the online form with your location, issue type and priority. A reference ID is issued instantly.
02
Triage
The Facilities Operations Centre reviews and assigns the appropriate trade team within 30 minutes.
03
Dispatch
The technician is dispatched. You receive an SMS update. Status is updated live in the registry.
04
Close
On resolution, a closure report is generated and emailed to the requestor for acknowledgement.
